# Independent natural-flower visitor wind test — source-first protocol
**Frozen:** 2026-11-19 before inspecting the raw transect outcomes.
**Study status:** external real-data *exploratory* reanalysis; not an untouched prospective replication.
**Repository boundary:** ecological sidecar only; frozen TNOA MEE Paper 1, Crall 2020, REC and C2 are unchanged.

## Scientific question
When wind conditions vary in a naturally flowering crop, do **how many** visitors arrive and **which visitor guilds** arrive exhibit different statistical responses? They are not equivalent ecological quantities. A null or adverse composition test is not proof that wind selectively suppresses every guild, and two different p values are not themselves a formal test of a contrast.

## Independent public natural-flower source
Karbassioon A & Stanley DA (2023), *Basic and Applied Ecology* 72:74–81, DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.baae.2023.06.001, collected 2021 in flowering oilseed rape (*Brassica napus*) in 3 fields, County Carlow, Ireland. Each field visited six times and surveyed in three fixed 15-minute transects per visit across daylight hours. Observers logged **actual floral visitors** within 1 m of the observer (honeybees, bumblebees, solitary bees, hoverflies; butterfly observed separately), including pollen-bearing bees. Temperature, humidity, radiation, rain and wind were averaged from a physical logger for the observation periods. One station was used and moved across fields; the authors deliberately selected relatively favourable weather.

Source archive: Zenodo record https://zenodo.org/records/10869660 (also indexed as Dryad DOI https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.jq2bvq8dw), file `1_osr_abundance_and_weather_data.csv`, published MD5 **85cc235a323d45c0fa52efcc27db9f82**. Pin the verified SHA256 only after original download. Do not commit the raw CSV to GitHub.

## Biological and measurement estimands
- **Amount**: sum of counts of the four guilds among observed natural flower visits per **15-minute transect**, including zero-visit transects. The 15-minute transect is an observation unit; field×date is the clustering unit.
- **Identity**: the four-guild composition conditional on an observed focal-guild visit, using all four guild counts for every nonzero transect. Pollen-bearing and non-pollen-bearing individuals of each bee guild are **combined**; recorded butterflies are reported separately and not silently merged into a guild.
- **Wind**: original `meanwindspd` m/s, within-field centred and scaled by its pooled IQR, continuous. Neither monotonicity nor direction is assumed. No source label is interpreted as TNOA N: wind is an ecological exposure and possible observation confounder, not automatically sensor noise.

## Admission gates (decide using schema and denominator support, before response associations)
1. Source MD5 agrees with publisher; all required columns present; no fabricated/deduplicated transects. Confirm unique `site × datetimestart × transect` and 15-minute effort from timestamps. If columns/row count are inconsistent, HOLD.
2. At least **36** eligible transects, **3** fields, **6** distinct dates, **80%** finite wind values, and wind pooled IQR above 0.1 m/s; no raw exposure outside physical plausibility. Missing wind/dropouts are reported, not imputed.
3. For four-guild composition, at least **three guilds** must each have at least **25 total visitors across >=10 transects in >=2 fields**. Retain all four original guilds if supported; if this fails, report **HOLD_COMPOSITION_SUPPORT** rather than redefining guilds after seeing wind effects. Zero-visit transects remain for the amount model.
4. Within-field×coarse-daypart permutation support: >=6 exchangeable strata, >=30 weather-qualified transects total in strata with >=2 transects; at least 3 distinct wind values in 2/3 fields. If not, inferential p is HOLD, though source-safe descriptive estimates may be reported.

## Frozen analytical questions, not pre-registered original study findings
**H1 (quantity)**: compare Poisson transect-count model `site fixed + field location edge/centre + quadratic solar-clock time` against same + within-site-centred wind; offset fixed log(15 min). Include all zero visits. Because weather was not randomised and overdispersion may be present, do not use naive Poisson standard errors as the primary evidence.
**H2 (composition)**: compare multinomial four-guild counts at transect grain, reduced model `site fixed + location + quadratic solar-clock time`, full + within-site-centred wind. Counts in the same transect stay together. No individual-pollinator pseudo-replication.
**H3 (weather sensitivity)**: repeat each with measured temperature and solar radiation, only if design rank and finite support permit. Separately examine whether wind varies within fields after conditioning on day/time; do not use extreme extrapolation.
**H4 (transport/context)**: contrast effect directions and qualified source domains with Crall 2020 male orchid bees *at synthetic scent lures* without calling the two datasets measurements of one identical estimand. A 2-study meta-analysis of natural flower-visitation effects is prohibited.

## Controlled inference
Use **999** random whole-transect wind permutations, fixed seed 20261119, within site × daypart (early/middle/late) when positive support exists. Site/daypart strata are defined by local observation hour BEFORE the biological outcomes. Preserve guild counts, time, site, centre/edge position, and zero counts; refit all eligible null models for each permutation. Report plus-one p and exact failed-optimization counts. The shuffle assumes exchangeability within groups and is not a randomized field experiment. If time/weather covariance breaks that assumption, downgrade inference rather than claim causality.

Report leave-one-field-out prediction sensitivity. With only 3 fields, excluding one field is a fragility analysis, NOT three independent replications. Include original study's established time-of-day guild differences as prior art, not a new discovery. Never claim that captured insects indicate pollen transfer effectiveness.

## Stop/decision rules
- If weather/guild support fails, HOLD. No altered group cutoffs or extra weather bins to rescue a positive result.
- If tests run, report both positive and nonsupportive results, per-field/clock sensitivity, effect sizes and confounding. No statement of "amount and identity differ statistically" without a formal cross-response contrast.
- If source is verified but non-transportable (different plant host, scent lure vs real flower), say so explicitly. Additional independent natural flower datasets are needed for broad ecological generality.
