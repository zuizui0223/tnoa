# TNOA novelty positioning

Status: **updated after targeted prior-art audit and D5 random-split specificity control**.

This document does not make an absolute historical-priority claim. The prior-art search is targeted rather than systematic.

## 1. What TNOA does not claim

TNOA does not claim to invent:

- abstention or reject-option prediction;
- uncertain, ambiguous or partial ecological observations;
- nondetection-is-not-absence;
- continuous-score ecological inference;
- multilabel coexistence or partial refusal;
- explicit ignorance/conflict representations;
- sensor fusion;
- information ordering/garbling;
- partial-identification bounds;
- formal risk-control or calibration theory.

These are established neighbouring ideas.

## 2. Residual methodological object

TNOA is an **upstream process-semantic observation contract** for ecological sensing. Its evidence propositions are deliberately heterogeneous:

- T: positive target support;
- N: separate positive nuisance-process support;
- O: measurement support rather than target confidence;
- C: a target-coupled local response that requires independent attribution before promotion to target support;
- A−: optional independently supported target absence.

T and N are non-complementary and may coexist. Low T does not create A−. The minimal B/T/N/U output is an observation vocabulary, not a set of mutually exclusive ecological classes.

The novelty claim is therefore functional rather than combinatorial: **define a process-semantic record upstream of ecological inference, calibrate its operational supports against declared error semantics, and measure the downstream information lost when the core record is coarsened.**

## 3. Strongest empirical pillars

### C6/C7 — representation change and decision calibration

A changed nuisance representation retained ranking while the inherited raw threshold lost its registered operating meaning. A predeclared family-conditional false-attribution criterion was then calibrated and checked held-out. This is a non-trivial observed failure/recovery sequence, not merely a consequence of the TNOA ontology.

### D1/D4 — core observation-state information loss

B/T/N/U versus target/not-target coarsening is the central downstream information result. The deterministic never-wider direction is Blackwell-style structure; the empirical contribution is the magnitude and conditions of the loss under the frozen six-regime emission map, including rare-target and bounded composition-weight sensitivity.

This result does not depend on claiming that any particular U-reason semantics are uniquely informative.

### C2 — preregistered negative result

The predicted narrow matched-timescale ambiguity ridge was not supported and was retained as a negative result.

## 4. D3/D5 is a self-critical supporting result, not a novelty pillar

D3 showed that splitting the frozen U column into two observed reason buckets sharply narrowed compatible latent mixtures. D5 then tested whether that gain was specific to the meanings of those two reason labels.

It was not. For target prevalence, the frozen two-reason split gave median width `0.004078`, while 500 unlabeled regime-dependent random two-way splits had median `0.005008`; `48.0%` of random splits were equal to or narrower than the frozen split. Random three-way splits generically reached full rank in the six-regime system.

Therefore Paper 1 must not claim a semantic-specific information premium for the selected U reasons. The defensible interpretation is:

> Additional non-redundant observation columns can improve identifiability in the frozen six-regime design; the current experiment does not isolate an information advantage caused by the selected reason semantics.

Reason provenance may still be scientifically useful when its semantics are independently defensible, but D3/D5 does not establish that semantic value quantitatively.

## 5. Frozen reasons are not the reusable API reasons

The frozen V14b surface contains two U buckets: `INFORMATION_ABSENT` and `OVERLAP_OR_ATTRIBUTION`. The later reusable API exposes four U reasons: no-supported-evidence, target+nuisance overlap, missing attribution and insufficient observability.

The frozen `OVERLAP_OR_ATTRIBUTION` bucket combines multiple mechanisms and there is no separate frozen insufficient-observability column. D3/D5 therefore do **not** validate a one-to-one four-reason API mapping.

## 6. Strongest safe novelty statement

> **TNOA contributes a tested upstream ecological observation contract that keeps target support, nuisance support, measurement observability, attribution-gated coupled response and independently supported absence as distinct propositions; documents an operational threshold-portability failure and error-criterion recalibration; and quantifies under frozen known truth the ecological identifying information lost when the core B/T/N/U record is garbled to binary.**

This wording deliberately does not claim that finer reason labels possess a demonstrated semantic-specific information premium.

## 7. Safe manuscript language

Preferred:

> Existing methods address imperfect detection, uncertain events, abstention, continuous classifier scores and partial identification. TNOA addresses the upstream sensor-interface problem of what process-semantic observation record should be retained before those methods are applied.

Preferred empirical framing:

> The core information result compares B/T/N/U with its binary coarsening. Finer U-reason refinements are reported as post-freeze diagnostics and are interpreted together with a random-split control showing that their additional narrowing is not semantic-specific in the frozen design.

## 8. Prohibited language

Do not write:

- “TNOA is the first abstaining ecological classifier.”
- “No previous method separates these quantities.”
- “TNOA introduces nondetection-is-not-absence.”
- “TNOA uniquely represents ignorance or conflict.”
- “The U-reason meanings themselves explain the 86% additional narrowing.”
- “The frozen D3 validates the current four-reason API.”
- “Reason provenance has a demonstrated semantic-specific information premium.”
